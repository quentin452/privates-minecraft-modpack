📦 Biggess Pack Cat Edition (Forge 1.16.5)

An Extreme Modded Adventure – Over 660 Mods!

🖥️ Recommended System Info:

    ✅ Play on Linux for significantly better performance

    ✅ At least 14 GB of installed RAM is highly recommended to avoid crashes during game startup

    ✅ Consider using Java 8 Semeru if the modpack consumes too much memory — it uses less RAM but may impact performance

🧭 Modpack Overview:

Embark on a massive modded journey with Biggess Pack Cat Edition, a heavily customized modpack for Minecraft 1.16.5 (Forge) featuring over 700 handpicked mods. This pack completely reimagines the vanilla experience with:

    🌍 New dimensions, biomes, and structures

    ⚔️ Challenging mobs, bosses, and dungeons

    🪄 Deep magic systems and advanced technology

    🧭 Rich exploration and immersive questlines

This modpack is designed for experienced players looking for a true challenge. The world is unforgiving—venturing into caves or braving the night without solid gear is almost a guaranteed death sentence.
🔑 Key Features:

    🔹 Over 700 mods (Forge 1.16.5)

    🔹 A mix of magic, technology, survival, and exploration

    🔹 High difficulty curve – balanced for veteran players

    🔹 Resource-heavy – intended for high-end systems

    🔹 BETA version – actively updated and improved

⚠️ Important Notes:

    ❌ Optifine is not supported

    ❌ Dedicated server version is not yet available (coming soon)

    ⚠️ This is a very heavy modpack – powerful hardware and a properly configured launcher (with sufficient RAM) are essential.
